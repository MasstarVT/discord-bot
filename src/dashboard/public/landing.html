Every tool your server needs, frozen into one bot.

THE MODULE TABLE

modules. One switch each.

Every feature is a module. Switch it on with /module or one click in the dashboard; whatever you leave off stays out of your way.

WEB DASHBOARD

Change any setting from your browser.

Log in with Discord, pick a server, and every module's options are there: channels, roles, messages, limits and schedules. Saved embeds, custom commands, reminders and feeds are lists you can edit too.

Beyond the usual.

  • Auto-Translate

    Translate a channel's messages into up to five languages as they're posted.

  • Counting

    Members count up from 1 in turns. A wrong number or a double turn starts it over.

  • Scrabble and more

    A full two-player Scrabble game, plus tic-tac-toe, rock-paper-scissors and number guessing.

  • A server economy

    A wallet per server: /daily streaks, work shifts, a shop, and blackjack, roulette and slots.

Up and running in three steps.

  1. STEP 01

    Add Frost.Byte

    Invite the bot to a server where you have Manage Server.

  2. STEP 02

    Turn on modules

    Run /module enable tickets or flip the switch in the dashboard.

  3. STEP 03

    Fine-tune on the web

    Log in with Discord and set channels, roles, messages and limits for each module.

Slash commands, not syntax.

Type /help in your server to see every command you can use there.

  • /rankShow your rank card or a member's.
  • /ticket-panelSet up ticket panels, managers, categories and transcripts.
  • /giveawayRun a giveaway with a Participate button.
  • /reactionroleRole panels with buttons, a dropdown or emoji reactions.
  • /pollCreate single or multiple choice polls.
  • /feedRSS, YouTube, Reddit, Bluesky and podcast alerts.
  • /dailyClaim your daily coins and keep a streak going.
  • /automodFilters for bad words, spam, invites, links and honeypot channels.
